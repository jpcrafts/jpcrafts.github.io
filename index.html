01 / Research

From the detector
to the measurement.

My work connects experimental hardware, reconstruction, and physics analysis across Jefferson Lab and the Electron–Ion Collider detector program.

01

Jefferson Lab · Hall C

Neutral-pion electroproduction with NPS

Developing the NPS RG1-A analysis toward semi-inclusive and exclusive π⁰ cross sections, from two-photon reconstruction and background subtraction to data–simulation comparisons.

  • Offline replay and analysis campaigns
  • HMS calibration and optics studies
  • Detector assembly, operations, and refurbishment

Physics analysis
02

Electron–Ion Collider · ePIC

Precision calorimetry and crystal QA

Developing PbWO₄ receiving-QA procedures for the ePIC electron-endcap electromagnetic calorimeter, with optical and dimensional measurements, inspection documentation, and crystal-level traceability.

  • QA contribution to the first 1,069-crystal procurement
  • Final-design-review materials and test procedures
  • EEEMCal beam tests at DESY in 2025
2026 ePIC/EICUG presentation ↗
Instrumentation
03

Jefferson Lab · Hall D

CompCal and lead-tungstate detector development

Designed calorimeter modules, assembly procedures, and a light-monitoring system for the PrimEx-η Compton calorimeter. Subsequent work included FCAL2/ECAL development and magnetic-shielding studies.

  • Crystal testing and detector commissioning
  • Hall D run coordination in Spring 2019
  • Authored detector studies and a 2021 CCAL paper
Read the CCAL paper ↗
Detector development
04

Current collaborative work

Streaming detector data and GPU processing

Contributing NPS data preparation, waveform-format and channel-mapping studies, and technical materials for FPGA-to-GPU readout development using DAQIRI and NVIDIA Holoscan.

This work also connects to the CUA-led DOE Genesis Phase I effort on stream-native, physics-integrated AI.
