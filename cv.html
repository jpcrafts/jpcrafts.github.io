Experience

Neutral Particle Spectrometer and Hall C analysis

Doctoral research at The Catholic University of America and Jefferson Lab, 2021–present. Develop the NPS RG1-A neutral-pion analysis toward semi-inclusive and exclusive electroproduction cross sections.

  • Implement two-photon reconstruction, kinematic selections, accidental-coincidence and dummy-target subtraction, charge normalization, and invariant-mass and missing-mass diagnostics.
  • Coordinate offline replay and analysis campaigns; contribute to HMS subdetector calibration and optics analysis, data–simulation comparisons, and reproducible C++/ROOT workflows.
  • Contributed to NPS assembly, installation, operations, winter refurbishment, and deinstallation for the Fall 2023 and Spring 2024 RG1-A runs; maintained the primary run list and supported online replay.
  • Document detector and operational lessons for future running, including high-voltage connections, temperature monitoring, safety, access, and refurbishment planning.

ePIC EEEMCal development and crystal quality assurance

Develop and implement PbWO₄ crystal receiving QA at Jefferson Lab, combining vendor documentation with visual inspection, sampled dimensional and optical-transmission measurements, and component traceability.

  • Contributed to QA for the completed first procurement of 1,069 crystals and its continuation into subsequent production deliveries.
  • Prepare QA plans, inspection and test procedures, receiving records, and crystal-QA materials for the EEEMCal final design review.
  • Participated in EEEMCal prototype and electronics beam tests at DESY, Hamburg, in March and December 2025.

Hall D calorimetry and detector development

Worked with Hall D physicists and engineers beginning in Summer 2016 on the CompCal Compton calorimeter for the PrimEx-η experiment and subsequent detector-development work.

  • Designed detector modules and assembly procedures; tested PbWO₄ crystals before installation and designed and implemented the light-monitoring system.
  • Tested and calibrated the detector during commissioning and served as Hall D run coordinator for the final week of the Spring 2019 run.
  • Supported Hall D detector maintenance and repairs during 2020 accelerator downtime while contributing to FCAL2/ECAL upgrade development, including magnetic-shielding studies.
  • Contributed mechanical designs and scintillator characterization for EIC calorimetry, including a proposed scintillating-glass barrel calorimeter and subsequent ePIC detector work.

Streaming readout and scientific computing

Contribute NPS detector-data preparation, waveform-format and channel-mapping studies, and technical presentations for collaborative FPGA-to-GPU streaming-readout development.

  • Support the NPS data path around DAQIRI and NVIDIA Holoscan and prepare detector-focused materials for GPU-processing integration and performance evaluation with NVIDIA.
  • Contributed to the accepted CUA-led DOE Genesis Phase I proposal effort on stream-native, physics-integrated AI, including milestone and technical-meeting materials.
